## 2.3 Demand Estimation

The project adopts an assumed laundry-generation factor of:

$$
\boxed{3.0\ \frac{\text{kg}}{\text{bed}\cdot\text{day}}}
$$

The total estimated daily laundry demand is calculated using the following formula:

$$
D = N \times L
$$

Where:

- $D$ = Total daily laundry demand (kg/day)
- $N$ = Total number of beds (beds)
- $L$ = Laundry-generation factor (kg/bed/day)

Substituting the project values:

$$
D = 2,515 \times 3.0
$$

Therefore, the total estimated daily laundry demand is:

$$
\boxed{D = 7,545\ \text{kg/day}}
$$

This represents an estimated daily laundry processing requirement of **7,545 kg/day (7.545 tonnes/day)**.
