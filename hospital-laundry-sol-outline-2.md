# 7\. Graph/simple median model

This is where we move from straight-line geography to the **road network**.

For each candidate, we calculate:

$$
S_j=\sum_i w_i d_{ij}
$$

where:

- $w_i$ = hospital beds
- $d_{ij}$ = road distance/time between hospital $i$ and candidate $j$

The candidate with the lowest weighted distance becomes the graph-based optimum.

This is likely to be particularly interesting because:

- Hospital de Sabadell is heavily weighted.
- Terrassa has two significant hospitals.
- HGC is far southwest.
- Barberà sits between these groups.
